# Donation and inquiry launch checklist

The current Giving page is an inquiry page, **not** an online payment processor. Do not claim that visitors can make a completed online gift until a payment provider has been connected and tested.

## Before activating online donations

- Confirm the Institute's legal name, entity and current tax status; publish only verified documentation and carefully reviewed deductibility language.
- Choose and connect an authorized nonprofit-compatible payment provider, configure the payout account and receipt sender, and verify transaction fees and any recurring-gift features.
- Create one-time and monthly giving options and verify secure provider-hosted checkout on mobile and desktop. Never collect card numbers in the static site or by email.
- Test successful, failed, canceled, and recurring gift flows and donor receipts with the authorized account owner.
- Set up an accessible privacy notice and data retention process before deploying a website-native donor or inquiry form.
- Connect an authorized CRM or secure form endpoint, configure spam protection, and verify email delivery and acknowledgement. The current homepage form opens the visitor’s email client; it is not a server-backed submission.
- Obtain consent before publishing photographs of identifiable students or minors. Keep concept imagery clearly distinguished from actual program photography.

## Content to request

Verified mission and leadership information, program eligibility and locations, current enrollment availability, actual outcomes with dates and sources, real testimonials with permission, and authorized donation provider details.
