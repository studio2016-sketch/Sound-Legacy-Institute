# Sound Legacy Institute — Visual representation standard

Multicultural representation is a site-wide design requirement, not an isolated campaign.

- Show young musicians, families, educators, and mentors from varied cultural and ethnic backgrounds across the homepage, Programs, The Work, and Giving pages. Reflect varied musical traditions, instruments, generations, and community settings.
- Prioritize real, permissioned Institute photography when available. Approved concept images may communicate the vision, but must not be captioned or described as documentation of actual students, classes, outcomes, or events.
- Preserve dignity and individual artistry: avoid tokenistic one-person-per-group layouts, stereotypes, or treating any culture as a visual accessory.
- When commissioning or generating new images, maintain consistent cinematic editorial quality, realistic musical technique, authentic instruments, warm natural expressions, and a variety of settings. Avoid duplicating the same faces or inventing specific real participants.
- Include accurate, useful alt text focused on the scene rather than guessing an individual's identity, nationality, ethnicity, or disability.
- Check consent and publication permissions for any identifiable minors before adding documentary photography.
- Maintain accessible contrast, responsive crops that retain faces and instruments, descriptive captions, and optimized local assets.
- Use the approved imagery in `images/` as the current starting point; keep the three existing local photos unless specifically replaced. Update the shared multicultural feature across all four public pages when imagery changes.
